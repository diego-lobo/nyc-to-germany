Die Orte · Basic information

Two doors to Germany in Manhattan

Most "German New York" sites look back at immigrant history. These two look forward: both exist to send students and researchers between New York and German universities. I use them to answer a practical question: which route should I take for a fall semester in Germany?

NYU German Department & Deutsches Haus → NYU Berlin

NYU's Department of German, at 19 University Place, teaches German from the very first semester. It offers a major, a minor, joint majors and graduate degrees, and it strongly encourages majors to spend a semester at NYU Berlin. A few steps away, Deutsches Haus at NYU opened in 1977 in a restored 19th-century coach house on Washington Mews. The building once held NYU medical labs and later the studio of the sculptor Paul Manship. Its founding director, Volkmar Sander, began raising money in 1966 with the help of a $100,000 matching grant from West Germany's foreign office. Today the Haus calls itself "New York's leading institution for culture and language of the German-speaking world." It runs public German classes, readings, a writers-in-residence program (since 1981), the Festival Neue Literatur (since 2010) and a children's program.

NYU Berlin, NYU's study-away site in the KulturBrauerei in Prenzlauer Berg, completes the path. It teaches humanities and social-science courses, offers special tracks and has close ties to Humboldt-Universität. Together the three take a student from a first German class in Manhattan to a semester in Berlin.

University of Cologne New York Office

The University of Cologne opened its New York Office in early 2011 as its representation in North America. It sits in German House at 871 United Nations Plaza, which also houses the German Consulate General, Germany's UN Mission and the New York offices of the DAAD and UAS7. The office builds partnerships with North American universities, hosts delegations, runs a New York summer school, stays in touch with alumni, and is the first point of contact for American and Canadian students who are interested in Cologne. It is also a main supporter of the German Center for Research and Innovation (DWIH New York). The partners it lists include NYU Stern and NYU's Deutsches Haus.

The university behind it is one of Europe's oldest. It was founded in 1388, closed under French rule in 1798 and re-founded by the citizens of Cologne in 1919. Its tie to NYU is very new: in August 2026, Rector Joybrato Mukherjee and NYU President Linda G. Mills signed a strategic partnership at Bobst Library. It covers research, teaching and exchange.

Beziehung zu Deutschland · Relationship to Germany

Back to the source of the research university

Studying in Germany means going back to the model behind the American research university. The University of Berlin (today's Humboldt-Universität) was founded in 1810 under the influence of Wilhelm von Humboldt. It rested on the Einheit von Lehre und Forschung (the unity of teaching and research) and on academic freedom for teachers and students (Lehr- und Lernfreiheit). American universities, beginning with Johns Hopkins, adopted these German principles early, and NYU's own research mission comes from the same tradition.

Academic exchange between the two countries is organized above all by the DAAD (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst). It was founded in Heidelberg in 1925 on the initiative of a single student. Today it describes itself as the world's largest organization of its kind and has supported nearly two million students and researchers. Its New York regional office, opened in 1971, is in the same German House as the Cologne office.

German student life differs from American student life in ways that matter for planning. The academic year is split into a Wintersemester (lectures from mid-October to February) and a Sommersemester (mid-April to July), so a German "fall" term runs into February. State universities generally charge no tuition, only a Semesterbeitrag of about €70–430, which often includes a transit ticket. Local Studierendenwerke run the Mensa and the residence halls, although many students share a flat (WG) instead. Courses also rely more on independent study, with grades often based on one exam or paper at the end of the term.

Beziehung zu New York · Relationship to NYC

From Kleindeutschland to UN Plaza

German New York began with immigrants. From the 1840s, Kleindeutschland on the Lower East Side and in the East Village, centered on Tompkins Square, was the city's German quarter. By 1855 New York had the third-largest German-speaking population of any city in the world, after Berlin and Vienna. The neighborhood declined after the General Slocum steamboat fire of June 15, 1904, which killed about 1,000 people from the community, mostly women and children. Families moved to Yorkville, and anti-German feeling during World War I ended the enclave.

The second wave was academic. After 1933 the Nazis expelled Jewish and dissenting professors, and Alvin Johnson of the New School created the University in Exile. By October 1933 ten scholars had arrived. It later became the New School for Social Research. The Emergency Committee in Aid of Displaced German (later Foreign) Scholars, formed in New York in 1933, supported 335 scholars.

After 1945 the relationship became official cultural diplomacy. Goethe House opened in 1957 and is today the Goethe-Institut at 30 Irving Place. Deutsches Haus at NYU moved into 42 Washington Mews in 1977, next to NYU's La Maison Française on the same mews. In the mid-1990s Germany built the 23-story German House at First Avenue and 49th Street. It now holds the Consulate General, the UN Mission, the DAAD, the German Center for Research and Innovation (DWIH) and the Cologne office. A redesign by Sauerbruch Hutton and Studio Gang was announced in 2026. The two sites on this page show that change: German New York moved from an immigrant neighborhood, to refugee scholars, to state-supported exchange.

Weg 1 · NYU's own site

NYU Berlin

Kalender
Runs on the NYU calendar (fall 2026: late August to mid-December), so you are home for spring.
Deutsch
No German needed to apply. Everyone takes one German course at their level, and other courses are in English.
Credit
12–18 NYU credits, with no transfer step. Some students also take 1–2 courses at Humboldt-Universität.
Kosten
Normal NYU tuition, and NYU financial aid applies. Add about $13,000–17,000 per semester for housing, food, flights and more. Scholarships: Global Pathways (up to $4,000, need-based), Study Away Grant (up to $3,000), Merit (up to $1,500).
Wohnen
NYU housing is required: a John Hejduk building in Kreuzberg, mostly shared doubles, 30–35 minutes from class.
Frist
Fall 2027 priority deadline: 15 Jan 2027. After that, applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
Pro / Contra
+ Simplest route: NYU credit and aid, US calendar, German built in. − Small American group, mostly English-taught classes, full NYU tuition.
Status: Available now. The fall 2027 interest form is open. An older German Department page still lists a German proficiency requirement, so I need to confirm with Global Programs.
Weg 2 · Direct at a German university

Universität zu Köln

Kalender
Wintersemester 2027/28 lectures run 11 Oct 2027 to 4 Feb 2028. That clashes with NYU's spring term. The business faculty offers an "early leaver" option (to 15 Dec), but only on nomination by the home school.
Deutsch
B2 in English or German recommended. The business faculty has 130+ English-taught courses.
Kosten
No tuition. Semester fee about €356, which includes a Germany-wide transit ticket. Living costs about €1,200/month, plus about €150/month health insurance.
Wohnen
Kölner Studierendenwerk halls (apply 1–2 semesters ahead, often full) or private rooms at €550–800.
Fristen
Nomination by 1–15 May 2027, application by 30 May to 5 June 2027.
Pro / Contra
+ Full immersion, no tuition, an NYU contact office in New York. − Exchange is for partner-university students only, and there are no "freemovers" at the business faculty.
Status: NYU is not yet on Cologne's exchange partner lists. Whether the August 2026 NYU–Cologne partnership opens undergraduate places for fall 2027 is unconfirmed. I need to ask NYU Global Programs and the Cologne NY Office.
Weg 3 · Study + internship / funding

UAS7 & Gilman–DAAD

Was?
UAS7 is seven German universities of applied sciences, including TH Köln. It offers a one-semester Study Program and a one-year Study & Internship Program (SIP: classes in winter, internship in spring).
Wer?
Full-time US sophomores and juniors in engineering, sciences, business, architecture or social work, with a GPA of about 3.0. You don't need to come from a partner school.
Deutsch
None needed for English-taught classes. B1 for German-taught classes or a self-arranged internship.
Kosten
No tuition. Semester fee about €300–360, halls about €300/month. Gilman–DAAD: up to $5,000 plus insurance for Pell Grant recipients.
Fristen
UAS7: 15 Feb 2027 for fall 2027. Gilman (fall 2027 cycle): 25 Feb 2027.
Pro / Contra
+ English options, built-in internship. − Limited fields, and the semester still runs past December.
Note: DAAD's RISE Germany is a summer research internship for STEM students (deadline 30 Nov 2026). It is a good alternative if a fall semester doesn't work.
